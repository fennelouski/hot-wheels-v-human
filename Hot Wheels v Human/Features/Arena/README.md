# Features/Arena/ — the 3D race scene (Phases 1–2 solo, 3 on TV)

Files
- `ArenaView.swift` — the `RealityView`: builds scene from `AppModel` blueprint via `TrackSpawner`, spawns cars via `CarFactory`, registers `DriveSystem`/`RaceRulesSystem`, directional light, inline chase camera (see below). Hosts overlay HUD. The `RealityView` alone takes `.ignoresSafeArea()` — scene edge to edge (under the iPad status bar, out to the TV's overscan), HUD and PiPs inside the safe area.
- `ArenaEnvironment.swift` (moved to `Core/RaceCore/` — no SwiftUI, and RaceSession now builds the world itself so it's ready BEFORE the countdown; the async re-theme here lagged behind it and races opened on the lobby's green field) — the world around the track: procedural sky dome (gradient + puffy clouds on candy/day/sunset, stars on space — both drawn chunky because the chase cam only ever samples a thin band of a texture that wraps 360°; clouds are redrawn a texture-width to each side so the seam can't slice them) + 90 m checkered play-mat ground + scattered toy props (item-* USDZ), themed per trackId (candy/day/sunset/space — order is load-bearing, byte-sum % `hashedThemeCount`, which stays 4: worlds appended after those are pick-only, so adding one can't re-theme existing tracks). **Pickable worlds** (Big City / Hometown / Park, Kenney city+nature kits at 0.3 scale) ride `TrackBlueprint.worldTheme` (additive optional on the wire) → `RaceSession.worldTheme` → `make(for:theme:around:)`; `structured` themes snap props to a 0.7 m street grid, one per cell, yaw squared toward the track — random-yaw buildings read as knocked over. Rebuilt per race via `RaceSession.trackID`/`trackFootprint`/`worldTheme`/`scenery`; props stay out of the track's footprint and carry no collision. The ground is a low-poly **terrain heightfield** (flat under the track + prop ring, rolling hills beyond, a mountain rim at the world's edge; the physics floor stays a flat invisible box), each theme rings **horizon silhouettes** (`Theme.horizon`, ~14 oversized clones at radius 31–40 m), and `RaceTuning.groundlessThemes` (space) skips the visual ground entirely — the track floats, `RaceSession` strips the `support-*` legs, and horizon props float at varied heights. Hand-placed `SceneryItem`s from the builder spawn via `SceneryPlacer` (Core/TrackKit). **The world moves**: `AmbientMotionComponent/System` (coins spin, ships/spacecraft/ghosts bob+roll, flags and trees sway — assigned by model name via `AmbientMotion`, applied to scatter, horizon, and hand-placed props alike), `WalkerSystem` strolls placed people, `TrainSystem` chugs Winter's 3-car toy train on an ellipse just outside the prop ring. City/Hometown/Speedway use `Theme.cityBlocks`: an AUTOTILED street grid (`streetTile(north:south:east:west:)` — crossroad/tee/bend/straight/dead-end chosen from neighbour connectivity, so roads read as roads even where the track punches through), buildings filling the blocks facing their street (600-entity cap for runaway tracks). `Theme.traffic` cars drive the grid via `TrafficSystem`: right-lane offset, stop-sign pause at intersections, braking for `WalkerComponent` pedestrians ahead, and a fade-out at dead ends after which the SAME car re-enters at a road START (a degree-1 cell, driving inward — `TrafficSystem.roadStart`). Placed vehicles (`SceneryPlacer.vehicles`, the Cars shelf) join traffic too: off-road they bee-line for the nearest connected cell (`seeking`), then drive the union of the world's auto grid and hand-laid `street-*` tiles (`handStreetCells`, 0.7 m snap); with no roads at all they wander leashed lazy curves (`WanderComponent`). City worlds also spawn **sidewalk strollers**: `PedestrianSystem` walks people along the road edges (street grid resampled at 0.35 m, `sidewalkCells`, ± side offset) joined with any hand-laid pavement into ONE network; each step past a building carries a 1-in-100 shop visit (walk to the door, fade in, browse, fade back out — `doorBeside` only fires on real building cells, auto blocks + hand-placed city buildings). Hand-placed people near any sidewalk join the same network with the same doors; cars brake for them too. Placeable sky-stuff (`SpaceStuff` in SceneryPlacer): procedural planets/moon/nebulae — no kit has them — floating at toy-sky height, slowly turning; nebulae are billboarded gradient quads. `TrackBlueprint.worldEmpty` skips ALL auto-placed stuff, keeping sky/terrain — the blank canvas option.
- `ArenaHUDView.swift` — TV overlay: countdown numerals, per-player name+lives banners, lap counter, finish banner, results panel (times, crashes, best segment, REMATCH prompt "press READY on iPad").
- Chase camera — inline in `ArenaView`'s update subscription (no separate `CameraSystem.swift`): follows the cars' midpoint, pulls back proportional to their separation, smooth lerp. **Loop swing:** a loop's circle lies in the plane of travel, so from straight behind it's edge-on and reads as a wall — while a car is on or nearing a loop (detected via `LaneFollowComponent.loopRanges`, lead-in `RaceTuning.loopCamLead`) the camera eases to a 3/4 side angle (`loopBias`) so the ring reads as a circle. Finish/crash special cams are not built yet. Solo Arena on iPad reuses everything.
- `SoloArenaView.swift` — iPad wrapper: full-bleed Arena with `RaceControlsView` floating over it (bottom-trailing, 24 pt inset — clear of the bottom-leading reaction-cam PiP and the bottom-centre race clock), wired via `LoopbackTransport`. Only the *buttons* come over from the dashboard — boost, cam, rematch; progress/lives/speed already live in the HUD's per-racer banner, and the full `DashboardView` column is still what the iPad shows in the Race-on-TV flow where there's no arena on the device. (This is Test Mode's home too — Test Mode = Solo Arena with `MatchConfig.mode == .test`, two local designs, no lives/boost). Also home to the **workshop "try it" kit**, since every use of it is "present a Solo Arena over what I'm building":
  - `.racePreview(isPresented:designs:blueprint:config:)` — full-screen cover with a labelled Close button, fresh race session per presentation (`runID`). Used by TrackBuilder, Customizer, CharacterEditor and TestMode so the gesture — big yellow button, race, X, keep building — is identical everywhere.
  - `TryItButton` / `SaveItButton` — the paired workshop buttons ("See it in 3D!" / "Test Drive!" + "Save it!"). Both `lineLimit(1).fixedSize()`: in a crowded toolbar SwiftUI would rather stack a label into "Se / e it / in / 3D" than overflow, which reads as a broken button.

  Workshops preview what's **on screen**, not what's saved — they pass `AppModel.stampedRaceDesign(car:driver:)` overrides and (for the builder) the live `model.blueprint`. That's the whole point: no save, no backing out.
- `VFX.swift` — boost tailpipe glow (emissive material pulse + particle if cheap), debris despawn fade, confetti burst on win (`ParticleEmitterComponent`).

Perf budget (Apple TV 4K): ≤ 40 track pieces + 2 cars + debris ≤ 150 draw-relevant entities; static track merged where possible; target steady 60 fps. Profile with RealityKit debug options before adding VFX. If PiP reaction cam (2nd RealityView) costs > 10% frame time → sprite fallback per ReactionCam/README.

# Features/TrackBuilder/ — 3D snap editor (Phase 5; thin UI over TrackKit)

Files
- `TrackBuilderView.swift` — layout: piece palette (bottom scroll), 3D scene up top with an overhead mini-map in the corner (a Button: tap toggles corner-size ↔ reading-size), toolbar. Toolbar = icon-only undo/clear/shuffle + **"Race it!"** + "Save it!". Icon-only is load-bearing, not styling: spelled out, five labelled buttons overflow an iPad in portrait and every one wraps into an unreadable stack (`Label` still hands the words to VoiceOver). "Race it!" races the blueprint *currently on the canvas* via `.racePreview` — no save required, because peeking mid-build is the point; "Save it!" is what persists the track and makes it race next.
- `TrackBuilder3DView.swift` — the main canvas: live RealityKit scene of the working track (real USDZ pieces via `TrackSpawner`, day-theme `ArenaEnvironment` world). One-finger drag orbits, pinch zooms (`#if os(tvOS)` gates the gestures, same split as `CarTurntableView`); camera aims at the footprint center with auto-fit distance, so framing keeps up as pieces snap on. Respawns on edit using the ArenaView holder-name dedupe pattern; spawn stays on the calling task (Task.detached inside RealityView closures never resumes).
- `TrackBuilderModel.swift` — owns working `TrackBlueprint`; the ONLY mutation API is `append(pieceType)` / `removeLast()` (`ghostIndices` rides along, index-keyed for the same reason `portalExits` is — append/removeLast only) — the open-exit constraint makes invalid tracks unrepresentable. Plus `selectWorld(_:)`/`toggleWorldEmpty()`: the header world chip opens a strip of every `ArenaEnvironment` theme plus the Empty card, stored as `blueprint.worldTheme`/`.worldEmpty`; the 3D scene rebuilds its world live (keyed on theme only — props don't re-scatter as pieces are added until the world is re-picked; they're collision-free decoration, so overlap is cosmetic). Live-validates via `BlueprintValidator`; publishes solver preview (2D projected path) for drawing.
- `PiecePaletteView.swift` — cards per `PieceType` (icon + name); cards gray out when appending them would fail validation (e.g. overlap) — try-validate on the fly. The row LEADS with the **Ghost** card (`circle.dashed`, `ghostToggle`): a sticky mode, not a one-shot, so a whole invisible stretch is one tap here then normal piece taps. Pieces appended while it's lit get `SegmentSpec.isGhost` and come out see-through in the builder (30%, `TrackSpawner.setOpacity`) and dashed on the mini-map — invisible only once the arena hides them. Ghosts are visuals-only: the bed, the spline and the checkpoints are identical, so the car drives the piece it can't see.
- `TrackCanvasView.swift` — the overhead schematic (mini-map + preset/saved thumbnails): colored segments per piece type, lane centerline, piece badges in full-size mode. Auto-fits the layout; no pan/zoom of its own — zoom = the mini-map size toggle.
- `BlueprintSyncButton.swift` — READY: final validate → `transport.send(.trackBlueprint(bp), reliably: true)` → waits for TV ack (`raceEvent`) → advances flow.

**Decorate mode** (the paintbrush toggle): the palette becomes the decoration box — every world's props (`DecorPaletteView`, world filter row + thumbnail row; thumbnails are pre-rendered PNGs in `Resources/Thumbs`, loaded by explicit bundle URL because named-image lookup missed loose files). Extra shelves: **Streets** (road tiles snap to the FULL 0.7 m traffic grid so hand-laid roads join the street graph; paths snap to a half tile; both place unrotated so they butt cleanly), **Cars** (16 vehicles incl. firetruck/garbage truck/tractor; placed vehicles drive: they seek the nearest road — auto grid or hand-laid — join traffic, and respawn at road starts; NO roads → they wander lazy leashed curves near the placement spot, `WanderComponent`). People placed near ANY sidewalk — hand-laid pavement (`street-square` / `city-path-*`) or a city world's road edges — walk one joined 0.35 m network (`PedestrianComponent`; road cells add a ±0.26 side offset that fades out onto hand pavement), turn around at ends instead of fading, and share the city strollers' 1-in-100 shop-visit door list (auto blocks + hand-placed `city-house/shop/skyscraper`, `SceneryPlacer.handBuildingCells`); far from pavement they keep the little patrol and **People** (mini-characters with a baked walk clip; they patrol a little line from where they're placed — `WalkerComponent`). **Barrier roads** (`street-barrier-a/b`, composed dead-end tile + roadblock): placeable NPC roads where traffic TURNS AROUND instead of fading out (`TrafficComponent.barriers`). The piece shelf ends with **Portal**: one tap appends the ring PAIR (`portalIn` + `portalOut`), then the next ground tap places the exit ring (`placePortalExit`, validator-guarded; undo removes the pair). Tracks may DIG: hillDown below ground level is legal, and while the track has buried pieces the builder forces the terrain to ~30% opacity (`GroundFadeComponent.forced`) so the kid never fights the dirt. Tap a prop → tap the ground to place (`SceneryItem` on the blueprint, capped at `RaceTuning.maxSceneryItems`); tap a placed prop to pick it up (it lifts + scales) → tap to set down, or tap the HELD prop again to spin it a quarter turn; Undo removes the last placement. While decorating, one-finger drag PANS across the ground (orbit is build-mode's gesture) — you have to reach past the track to build a town. The world strip's **Empty** card (`TrackBlueprint.worldEmpty`, additive optional) keeps the theme's sky/terrain but none of the auto-placed stuff. The ground tap point comes from `TrackBuilder3DView.groundPoint` — our own frustum cast through `cameraPose` (iOS tap gestures are 2D; the targeted gesture only says WHICH entity was hit). Placed props are tap targets only in the builder (`SceneryPlacer.spawn(tappable:)`) — the arena spawns them collision-free.

UX guardrails (kid-first): no free placement/rotation for TRACK pieces — they always attach to the open exit with auto-derived orientation (this is why the builder is simple AND why the piece list has no rotation field). Decorations are the free-placement outlet, and they're cosmetic so nothing can break. Difficulty meter = count of loop/bump/ramp pieces as `flame.fill` SF Symbols (never emoji — CLAUDE.md). Empty state shows a ghost "start gate goes here" hint.

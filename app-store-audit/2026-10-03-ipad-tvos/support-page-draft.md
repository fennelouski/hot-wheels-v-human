# HWvH support

Prepared for the unlisted route `https://nathanfennel.com/hwvh/support.html`.
The standalone HTML is prepared in website commit
`b5a6148f09666b133b6d90155739731832671365` and must be checked against the final
verified native build. The route is not deployed or verified.

## Build, then race

Pick a player, make a character, customize a car and build a track. Use the
play button to try your creation. Close the preview to return to the workshop.
Saved profiles, characters, cars and tracks stay on the device where you
create them.

## Apple TV

Use the Siri Remote to move focus and select an action. The TV workshop has
remote controls for track pieces, decorations, car styles and character
choices. Menu returns from the current screen. A connected keyboard can help
with names. Camera photos and Apple Pencil drawing are iPad hardware tools;
TV styling uses remote controls. In Draw, switch the pen control between
Pen Up and Pen Down. With the pen down, select direction buttons to draw.
Lift the pen to move without making a mark. Eraser and Clear Drawing remove
marks.

## Race with nearby iPads

Open local multiplayer on Apple TV, then choose Race on TV on the iPad. Allow
Local Network when your device asks. Choose a car and tracks, then ready up.
Keep the devices nearby on a network that allows local peer connections.
If discovery fails, check Local Network permission in Settings. Guest or
managed Wi-Fi networks may isolate devices.

## The iPad camera is optional

Take a photo only when you want the app to pick your character colors. The
camera photo stays in memory on the iPad and is not saved or uploaded. You
can always choose colors manually.

## Supported devices

This release is for iPadOS 26 or later and tvOS 26 or later. It does not ship
an iPhone, Mac or Vision app. iPad and Apple TV have their own local saved
libraries; the app does not automatically sync those libraries through iCloud.

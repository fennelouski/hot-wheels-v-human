No sign-in is needed.

1. Tap Play on the opening screen to race standalone against a computer-driven car. Creating a player profile is optional.
2. Open the Workshop to style a racer, change car paint or edit a track. Save a design to keep it on this device.
3. Let a race finish normally and inspect the result. Choose Share to see the completed race card, then open the system share sheet. The card includes the actual arena image and race results. Dismiss the chooser and choose Rematch to race again.
4. Open Settings / Game Feel. Change Animation and Haptics independently, then relaunch to check that each choice stays saved. Animation respects the device's Reduce Motion setting; vibration depends on iPad hardware.

Nearby racing and TV-to-iPad image handoff require updated apps and Local Network permission on both devices. Discovery, the permission flow, image delivery and reconnect between a physical Apple TV and iPad have not yet been validated by the release owner.

The optional camera-color feature is unavailable in this version; manual racer styling is available.

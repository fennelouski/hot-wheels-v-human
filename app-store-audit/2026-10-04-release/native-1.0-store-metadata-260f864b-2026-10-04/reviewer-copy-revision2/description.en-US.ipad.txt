HWvH brings toy-car racing and a creative workshop to iPad and Apple TV.

Tap Play to start racing against a computer-driven car. Creating a player profile is optional, so you can get straight to the track.

Make a racer your own, change car colors and build tracks in the workshop. Save your designs on the device for the next race.

Finish a race, check the result and choose Rematch to go again. On iPad, Share exports a completed race card with the actual arena image, race results and the app link through the system share sheet.

Game Feel has separate saved Animation and Haptics choices. Animation respects Reduce Motion; vibration depends on iPad hardware.

No account, advertising or in-app purchases. Saved designs and preferences stay on the device; there is no automatic cloud library sync.

Requires iPadOS 26 or later. A separate Apple TV version requires tvOS 26 or later.

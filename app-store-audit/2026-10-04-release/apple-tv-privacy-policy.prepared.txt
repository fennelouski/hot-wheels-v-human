HWvH privacy policy

Updated October 4, 2026. Developer: Nathan Fennel. This policy covers the iPad and Apple TV app.

Your game stays with you

HWvH has no account system, advertising, analytics or developer-operated game server. We do not receive your profiles, drawings, saved designs or race results. The app keeps saved player profiles, character colors, cars, tracks and world decorations on the device where you create them. Animation and Haptics preferences are saved independently on that device. A completed race and its image are held locally for viewing and sharing.

Nearby races

When you choose local multiplayer, the app connects to nearby game devices using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework with encryption required. Other participants receive the player name and game information needed for that race, such as car and driver styles, drawing artwork, tracks, decorations, readiness, controller actions and race results. Discovery also uses a device display name. This information goes to nearby game participants, not our server.

Local network access is optional. Standalone building and racing do not require an internet account. Device-to-device games need nearby devices and local network access.

Sharing a finished race

A share image includes an actual frame of the finished game, the track name, car names, finish times, top speeds, crash counts and an App Store link. A nearby iPad can receive that frame and its result data over the encrypted local connection. For a standalone Apple TV race, the app starts a temporary sharing host only when you open the share panel and stops it when you close the panel. An existing multiplayer race uses its current connection.

The app holds received results in memory and clears them when the race restarts or you leave the connection. Requesting Get Race Image retries preparation for the same completed race. On iPad, preparing an image creates a PNG in the app's local cache; cached files may remain until the system clears the cache or you remove the app. The image is not automatically added to Photos or uploaded to a developer server. Share Image opens the system sharing sheet, and you choose whether and where to send it. A destination you choose has its own privacy practices, and copies you share remain with their recipients.

Character colors

Choose character colors manually in this release. Camera-based color picking is unavailable, so the app does not capture or analyze camera photos. Your chosen colors can become part of your saved driver and be shared as part of a local race. Apple TV does not request camera access.

Saved items and permissions

Use the app's delete controls to remove saved items you no longer want. You can manage Local Network permission in your device's Settings. Removing the app removes its local application data. Device backups and copies shared with other participants are separate. The app does not provide an online account or an automatic cross-device cloud library.

Website and support

Opening our website is separate from using the offline game. Web hosting may receive ordinary request information, such as an IP address, to serve a page. Information you choose to include in a support request is used to answer that request. The game does not automatically send support messages or diagnostics.

Contact

Contact Nathan Fennel (https://nathanfennel.com/contact) with privacy questions. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.

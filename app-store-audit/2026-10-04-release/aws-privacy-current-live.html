HWvH privacy policy

Updated October 3, 2026. Developer: Nathan Fennel. This policy covers the iPad and Apple TV app.

Your game stays with you

HWvH has no account system, advertising, analytics or developer-operated game server. We do not receive your profiles, drawings, saved designs or race history. The app keeps your player profiles, character colors, cars, tracks, world decorations and results on the device where you create them.

Nearby races

When you choose local multiplayer, the app connects to nearby game devices using Apple's encrypted Multipeer Connectivity framework. Other participants receive the player name and game information needed for that race, such as car and driver styles, drawing artwork, tracks, decorations, readiness, controller actions and race results. Discovery also uses a device display name. This information goes to nearby game participants, not our server.

Local network access is optional. Standalone building and racing do not require an internet account. Device-to-device games need nearby devices and local network access.

Optional iPad camera

On iPad, you can choose to take one photo to pick colors for a character. The app analyzes the image on the iPad using Apple's Vision framework. It does not save the original camera photo in your app library, add it to Photos or send it to another device. The resulting character colors can become part of your saved driver and be shared as part of a local race. You can choose colors manually instead. Apple TV does not request camera access.

Saved items and permissions

Use the app's delete controls to remove saved items you no longer want. You can manage Camera and Local Network permissions in your device's Settings. Removing the app removes its local application data. Device backups and copies shared with other participants are separate. The app does not provide an online account or an automatic cross-device cloud library.

Website and support

Opening our website is separate from using the offline game. Web hosting may receive ordinary request information, such as an IP address, to serve a page. Information you choose to include in a support request is used to answer that request. The game does not automatically send support messages or diagnostics.

Contact

Contact Nathan Fennel with privacy questions. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.

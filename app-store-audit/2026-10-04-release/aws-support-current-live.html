HWvH support

Developer: Nathan Fennel. Updated October 3, 2026.

Build, then race

Pick a player, make a character, customize a car and build a track. Use the play button to try your creation. Close the preview to return to the workshop. Save profiles, characters, cars and tracks on the device where you create them.

Use the Siri Remote

Move focus and select an action to edit tracks, decorations, car styles and characters on Apple TV. Menu returns from the current screen. A connected keyboard can help with names.

In the car's Draw section, select the pen control to switch between Pen Up and Pen Down. With the pen down, select direction buttons to draw. Lift the pen to move without making a mark. Choose an ink color and brush width, or use Eraser and Clear Drawing to remove marks.

Race with nearby iPads

Open local multiplayer on Apple TV, then choose Race on TV on the iPad. Allow Local Network when your device asks. Choose a car and tracks, then ready up. Keep the devices nearby on a network that allows local peer connections.

If discovery fails, check Local Network access in Settings. Guest or managed Wi-Fi networks may isolate devices. Standalone building and racing remain available without local multiplayer.

The iPad camera is optional

Take a photo only when you want the app to pick your character colors. The app analyzes the picture on the iPad and does not save it in your app library or upload it. You can always choose colors manually. Apple Pencil drawing is available on iPad; Apple TV uses its remote pen controls.

Supported devices and saved items

This release is for iPadOS 26 or later and tvOS 26 or later. iPad and Apple TV have their own local saved libraries. The app does not automatically sync those libraries through iCloud. Save changes before leaving an editor and keep any items you need before deleting local app data.

Get help

Contact Nathan Fennel with your device model, OS version, app version and the steps that went wrong. Include screenshots only if you are comfortable sharing their contents.
